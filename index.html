Research

Ongoing · DA-IICT · 2026

Digital Consumption & Academic Performance

with Srishti Lamba · supervised by Prof. Bhaskar Chaudhury

A longitudinal study examining screen-time reporting behaviour and academic performance among university students. What interests me most is not whether people use their phones more than they should, but whether we can even measure that behaviour accurately in the first place.

Most existing studies treat “screen time” as a single number. We disaggregate it by app category — social media, education, communication, entertainment — using objective device-exported logs rather than self-report, and formally test whether the relationship between digital consumption and academic performance is bidirectional.

Design
3-wave longitudinal · 14 weeks
Sample
n = 30–35 · DA-IICT students
Device data
StayFree exports — usage time, sessions, unlocks
Classification
Play Store metadata · TLD suffix rules · Cohen's κ
Scales
BSMAS · PHQ-4 · KSS · AMS
Models
Mixed-effects · cross-lagged SEM · latent growth · logistic/Lasso/RF
Tools
Python · R (lme4, lavaan)
Status
Ongoing

Key hypotheses

  • H9 — Wave-1 passive consumption predicts Wave-3 GPA independently of Wave-1 GPA.
  • H10 — Declining GPA predicts increased passive use (the avoidance hypothesis).
  • H11 — The relationship is bidirectional: both paths are significant simultaneously.
