Research Ongoing · DA-IICT · 2026
Digital Consumption &
Academic Performance

I am currently working on a longitudinal study examining screen-time reporting behaviour and academic performance among university students.

The project combines statistical modelling, survey methodology, and causal reasoning. What interests me most is not whether people use their phones more than they should, but whether we are even able to measure that behaviour accurately in the first place.

Most existing studies treat "screen time" as a single number. We disaggregate it by app category — social media, education, communication, entertainment — using objective device-exported logs rather than self-report, and test formally whether the relationship between digital consumption and academic performance is bidirectional.

with Srishti Lamba
·
supervised by Prof. Bhaskar Chaudhury
Study details
Design3-wave longitudinal · 14 weeks
Samplen = 30–35 · DA-IICT students
Device dataStayFree exports · usage time, sessions, unlocks
ClassificationPlay Store metadata · TLD suffix rules · Cohen's κ
ScalesBSMAS · PHQ-4 · KSS · AMS
ModelsLME · Cross-lagged SEM · LCGA · Logistic / Lasso / RF
ToolsPython · R (lme4, lavaan) · Mplus
StatusOngoing · May 2026
Key hypotheses
H9 — Wave-1 passive consumption predicts Wave-3 GPA independently of Wave-1 GPA
H10 — Declining GPA predicts increased passive use (avoidance hypothesis)
H11 — The relationship is bidirectional: both paths significant simultaneously
